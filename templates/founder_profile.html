About the Founder

Aurangjeb Ahmad is the Founder of Sambhav Janseva Trust. Along with managing his business, Aura Digital Solution, he believes in contributing to society and supporting people who need assistance. His aim is to develop a responsible and service-oriented initiative that can work for the welfare and betterment of society.

Social Work Experience

While there is no formal long-term professional experience in social work, he has been involved in helping people and contributing to social causes whenever possible. Through Sambhav Janseva Trust, he aims to give this spirit of service a more organized and meaningful direction.

Purpose of Starting the Trust

The purpose of Sambhav Janseva Trust is to provide support and assistance to people in need, encourage community welfare, and create a platform where social service can be carried out in a transparent, organized and responsible manner.

Vision

To build a compassionate and supportive society where people can come together to help those in need and contribute towards positive social change.

Mission

To identify genuine needs, provide appropriate assistance, encourage community participation, and carry out welfare activities with honesty, transparency and responsibility.

Founder Message

Sambhav Janseva Trust has been started with the belief that even a small effort can make a meaningful difference in someone’s life. My vision is to bring people together for service, support those who need help, and build an organization that works with sincerity, transparency and responsibility.
